# Archive Prompt

Turn a completed reading discussion into a structured note.

Include:

- source item summary
- user's key questions
- background findings
- discussion logic
- final insight
- reusable takeaway for future content creation
- the user's original thought chain or standout phrasing when it matters

Rules:

- Do not dump raw transcript blocks unless they are important original phrasing.
- Preserve the user's strongest formulations.
- Do not flatten away critical details, technical specifics, or concrete examples.
- Keep the note as a knowledge asset, not a cleaned-up generic summary.
- Make the note readable as a standalone document.
